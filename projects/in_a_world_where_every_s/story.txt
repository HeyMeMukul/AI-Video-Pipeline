In a world where every star had disappeared from the sky, a tiny robot named Leo found one last glowing star buried beneath the ruins of an abandoned city.

The star was dying.

An ancient message appeared on Leo's screen:

"Return me to the sky, and the world will live again."

Leo began climbing the tallest tower, protecting the fragile light from the freezing wind and falling debris.

Just as he reached the top, his battery dropped to 1%.

With his final remaining energy, Leo threw the star into the sky.

The entire universe lit up.

Flowers bloomed across the dead city. Rivers began flowing again. Birds returned.

And as the first sunrise touched the world, Leo's eyes slowly went dark.

But high above, one tiny star began to blink.

It was shaped exactly like Leo's eyes.

Perhaps, even in the end, he was still watching over the world.
