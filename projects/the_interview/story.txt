Title: THE INTERVIEW

Hook:
I walked into an executive interview with absolutely zero preparation.

Scene 1:
A broke, disheveled college student named Leo sits in an oversized leather chair across a sleek glass desk from Ms. Vance, a sharp senior corporate vice president. Ms. Vance peers over her designer glasses, clicks her pen, and asks:
"So, Leo... where do you see yourself in five years?"

Scene 2:
Leo leans back with completely unearned confidence, making unflinching deadpan eye contact. He replies:
"Honestly? In your position."
Ms. Vance arches an eyebrow, a faint cynical smirk breaking her poker face:
"Ambitious. And what exactly would you do if you got my position?"

Scene 3:
A pregnant awkward pause. Leo doesn't blink, deadpan delivery, completely flat and matter-of-fact:
"Hire someone to answer stupid questions."
Total deafening room silence. The fluorescent lights hum.

Scene 4:
Ms. Vance stares in dead silence, then slowly closes the heavy manila folder on her desk with an authoritative slap.
"You're hired."
Leo's stoic posture instantly collapses into sheer bewilderment:
"Wait... seriously?"

Scene 5:
Ms. Vance takes off her glasses, leans forward over the desk, dropping the corporate facade, and whispers:
"Yeah. We desperately need someone who can insult upper management without HR noticing."

Punchline:
Turns out... that was the qualification.
